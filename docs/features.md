# List of user roles and features

## Organizer

Oversees the organization of a specific tournament edition.

- Add, modify, or delete teams
- Add, modify, or delete games, including scores
- Send announcements to teams and players
- Import and export tournament data
- Send invites to unregistered players (so they can register an account)

## Team captain

Leads a team, and handles communication with other teams.

- Accept or decline game results proposed by the opponent team
- Propose game results to the opponent team

## Player

A non-captain player participating in some teams.

Team captains are also players, and so include their capabilities.

- View their tournaments and teams
- Receive announcements and notifications from the organizer

## Guest

A user that falls into none of the other roles.

All other user roles include the capabilities of this role.

- View tournaments, teams, players, and game results
- View tournament standings
