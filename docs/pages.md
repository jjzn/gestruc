# List of application pages

## Tournament page

Shows a list of teams and games (possibly as two separate tabs), as well as a
list of players for the tournament organizer. The organizer can also import and
export tournament data, send announcements, and add teams and game results.
Team captains can propose game results from this page.

Global announcements are also displayed on this page.

## Team page

Shows the team members and their played games in the associated tournament
(each team is unique to a specific tournament). Organizers can modify and
delete the team. Captains can propose game results and view their game results
pending their approval.

## Player page

Shows a player's tournaments, teams, and received notifications.

## Game page

Shows the results of a game, and allows team captains to approve or decline
proposed game results. Tournament organizers can modify or delete the game and
its scores.
